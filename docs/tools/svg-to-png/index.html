    Questions

    Are my SVG files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Your browser draws each SVG and makes the PNG inside this tab. The Content Security Policy sets connect-src to none, so the page cannot send your files anywhere. Open the Network tab of your browser tools and you will see no request when you convert.

    What size will the PNG be?

    The size the SVG asks for (its width and height, or its viewBox) times the scale you pick: 1x, 2x, 4x or 8x. Or type a width in pixels and the height follows the aspect ratio. An SVG with no size at all is drawn at 300 x 150, as browsers do. Very large sizes are made smaller to fit the browser limit of 16,384 pixels a side.

    Is the background transparent?

    Yes, by default the PNG keeps the transparent parts of the SVG. Pick White, Black or a custom color to fill the background instead.

    Can I convert many files at once?

    Yes. Drop or pick up to 200 SVG or .svgz files. Each one gets its own PNG and Download button, and Download all as ZIP saves them in one file. You can also paste SVG code, or paste an SVG you copied from a design tool.

    Why does my SVG look different?

    In a PNG the SVG is drawn as an image, so its scripts do not run, animations stop at the first frame, and fonts, pictures or styles linked from the web are not loaded. Use fonts installed on your computer or turn text into paths, and put pictures inside the SVG. An SVG with HTML inside (foreignObject) cannot be saved as PNG by the browser.

    Is a hostile SVG safe to open?

    Yes. The SVG is only ever drawn as an image, where browsers never run scripts or load outside files. Files that expand into millions of shapes through use elements, or declare XML entities, are refused before they are drawn.
