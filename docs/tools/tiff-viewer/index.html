Questions

Is my TIFF uploaded anywhere?

No. The page reads the file inside this tab, and only the parts a page needs. Its Content Security Policy sets connect-src to none, so the page cannot send your image anywhere. The TIFF reader (geotiff.js) runs in a background worker of the page: it loads once from cdn.jsdelivr.net at a pinned version, and its hash is checked before it runs. Your file is never sent there.

Which files does it open?

TIFF and BigTIFF, little or big-endian, strips or tiles, interleaved or planar: 1 to 32-bit integers and 32 or 64-bit floats, in gray, RGB, RGBA, palette, CMYK and YCbCr. Compression: none, LZW, Deflate, PackBits, JPEG, Zstd, LERC and WebP, with the horizontal predictor. CCITT fax, old-style LZW, JPEG 2000 and complex-number pages are listed with their tags and a note.

How does the contrast work?

8-bit pages open as stored. 16-bit, 32-bit and float pages open with a 0.5 to 99.5 percentile stretch, so a microscope or elevation image shows detail and not a black square. Switch to 2 to 98 %, min to max or the full type range. The value under the pointer is always the stored value, not the screen color.

What does it show for a GeoTIFF?

The EPSG code, pixel size, origin, bounds, the GDAL nodata value and every GeoKey by name. The pointer readout adds the map coordinate: easting and northing, or longitude and latitude. Nodata and NaN pixels are transparent and left out of the stats. There are no map tiles and no reprojection, so nothing is fetched.

How big a TIFF can it open?

The page has no file size limit of its own and reads only the strips or tiles of the page you look at. Pages up to 16 million pixels are decoded in full. A bigger page is decoded at a lower resolution that fits, and the page says so. A page that claims more than 2 billion pixels is refused, and its tags are still listed.

Is a hostile or broken TIFF safe to open?

Tag text is written as plain text, never as HTML, and the page allows only its own scripts and the pinned library. Pages are decoded in a background worker that is stopped after 30 seconds, so a broken file cannot freeze the tab. Pages that claim impossible sizes are refused, and a page list that loops back on itself is caught.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the page keeps a copy of its own files and of the pinned TIFF reader, so it opens files with no connection.
