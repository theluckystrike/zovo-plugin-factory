Questions

Is my book uploaded anywhere?

No. The page reads the EPUB inside this tab, and the EPUB Reader extension's own code opens it in a background worker of the page. The Content Security Policy sets connect-src to none, and the worker has no network functions. Open the Network tab of your browser tools and you will see no request when a book opens.

Which books does it open?

EPUB 2 and EPUB 3 books up to 200 MB, as sold without DRM by many shops and as made by Calibre, Sigil, Pandoc and InDesign. Books with Adobe or Apple DRM cannot be read by any web page. Kindle files (MOBI, AZW) need to be converted to EPUB first.

Can I change the font and theme?

Yes. Pick paged or scrolled reading, the font size, serif or sans, and a light, sepia or dark theme. Your choices stay in this browser for the next book.

Can I search the book?

Yes. Search looks through every chapter and lists the matches with a little context; click one to jump there. Footnotes open in a popup, so you keep your place.

Is a book with scripts safe to open?

Yes. Every chapter goes through an allow-list cleaner before it is shown: scripts, frames, forms, event handlers and outside resources are removed, and the page allows only its own scripts. Pictures come from inside the book, never from the web.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the page keeps a copy of its own files, so it opens books with no connection.
