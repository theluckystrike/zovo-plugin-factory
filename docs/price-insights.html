Price insights

Zovo Pro price research, 20 Nov 2026. No live price changed. The full research is on the Pricing tab, and the ready /goal v9 command is in its section 8.

Price to set

  • Personal USD 29 once. Show the date when it rises to USD 39 (1 Jan 2027).
  • Business USD 59 per seat, with an invoice and the buyer's tax ID. Rival business seats cost 2x the personal price. Today the Team pack works out to USD 11.80 a seat.
  • Expected gain is small. About USD 20 to 60 a month now, and about 3x that once installs triple.

Biggest insights

  1. Price is not what stops people. The model's revenue barely changes between USD 19 and USD 59 (about 1 % up or down). Nobody clicked "Too expensive" (0 of 11 "Not now" clicks).
  2. Only the 7-day trial dialog sells. 2 of the 4 people who came from it paid. 0 of 8 from other places did. Putting it on the top 30 listings is the biggest lever.
  3. The "rivals charge USD 99" figure was wrong. It mixed in desktop apps. The real median one-time price for VS Code extensions is USD 31.49. So USD 19 is underpriced, but not by 5x.
  4. Trust is weaker than value. The Pro offer scores 56 of 100 (content 78, trust 29). There are 4 ratings across 136 listings and 4 different seller names. zovo.one also sells a different "Zovo Pro" (the Chrome one) at USD 99.
  5. 84 % of the top-20 installs face a strong free alternative. Hex Viewer has 1 install for every 4,240 of Microsoft's free hex editor. The money is in database tools and engineering niches like SQLite, ELF/PE and CAN DBC.

Decisions only the owner can make

  • New Stripe links. Create them for USD 29 Personal and USD 59 per Business seat.
  • Sponsors tier. Move the USD 19 GitHub Sponsors tier to USD 29. It also hands out Pro keys, so it would undercut the new price.
  • One seller name. Switch to Stripe Managed Payments (3.5 % fee) or Polar. Either one also handles UK VAT, which is owed from the first UK sale.
  • Stop rule. If fewer than 15 % of 30 checkouts at USD 29 pay, go back to USD 19.
