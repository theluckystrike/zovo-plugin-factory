Geo is #1 on 4 phrases with 0 installs. Make each kept product the best on its platform for a phrase with real demand before building new ones.
  2. Wave13 correction: new listings do not settle in 1 hour. Ranks keep improving to 7 hours. We used to read the rank 1 hour after release and treat it as final, because the small-niche batch of 7 November did not move from 1 hour to 7 hours (RANK9). RANK10 ranked the 7 listings of 8 November again 5 to 7 hours after release (F). On 37 phrases, #1 went from 4 to 8, top 3 from 9 to 13 and top 10 from 21 to 27. The hex viewer climbed from #17 to #43 at 1 hour to #4 to #12, and had 13 installs. So the 1-hour rank is a first reading only. Judge a new listing or a name change at 7 hours or later, and do not drop a listing for its 1-hour rank.
