Biggest insight (8 November 2026)

Rank wins, not product count. The factory has $0 revenue. On 8 November at 18:13, 36 live VS Code listings had 203 installs (118 at 08:33, 55 at 19:01 on 7 November). Installs come almost entirely from a top-3 rank on a phrase people search for. DOCX Viewer has 43 of the 203. The new hex viewer got 15 in its first 5 hours, because "hex editor" has big demand. The other 6 new listings got 3 together. More products in thin niches do not change that. Rank on phrases with real demand does.

Our standing rule: for every product we keep, the goal is to be the best one on its platform for its main phrase: the features users ask rivals for, a first screen that shows them, real screenshots, a name that has every word of the phrase, and ratings. A new niche must have a top-5 rank on a phrase where rivals get at least 150 installs a day. Judge each change 7 hours after release or later.

We rate wave12 so far (8 November 08:40 to 18:20) 60 out of 100. Installs went from 118 to 203. 7 of 7 new listings and 27 updates went live with 0 failed runs, and the red team stopped 4 crash-class defects first. But revenue is $0, ratings are 0 on 36 listings, no money gate moved, 5 of the 7 new names were taken by other publishers, and free disk at 18 to 20 GB stopped the release checks from 16:51, so 14 staged updates for the 22:03 window still have no PASS row.
