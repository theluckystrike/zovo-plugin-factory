Five key insights, 9 November

  1. Niche beats polish. One listing in the right niche, the Hex Viewer, has 175 installs in 35 hours (live since 8 November 13:13). A full day of README, rating-ask and cross-sell releases on the other listings moved the top 3 by about 1 percent. Pick the niche first.
  2. Demand beats rank. The Geo map viewer is #1 on 4 phrases and has 5 installs, because few people search those phrases. Build only where rivals together gain 150 or more installs a day and the leader is weak.
  3. Renames can lose. XLSX fell from #15 to #24 on "excel viewer" and from #13 to #25 on "csv viewer" after its rename, and PDF fell from #2 to #7 on "pdf reader". Keep the old winning words when you add new ones, and check every new name against phrase demand before release.
  4. Ranks keep improving for about 7 hours. After a release, top-3 places on the measured phrases went from 9 at 1 hour to 13 at 7 hours (37 phrases). Judge a release at 7 hours, not at 1 hour.
  5. Keep the search phrase together. Names that keep the exact search phrase together and first rank #1 to #10. Names that split or reorder it rank #14 to #60 (NICHE6, 13 listings). The 5 listings of 9 November with split names get the first rename-only fixes.
