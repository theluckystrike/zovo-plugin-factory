Privacy policy for Zovo Hide Shipping & Payment

Last updated 9 November 2026.

Zovo Hide Shipping & Payment ("the app") is made by Zovo (theluckystrike on GitHub). This page says what data the app handles and why.

What the app stores

The app stores your rules in one metafield on each of the two checkout customizations it creates in your store (one for shipping, one for payment). These metafields live in your Shopify store, not on our servers. They hold rule settings that you type in, such as method names, cart values, countries, postal code prefixes, customer tags and product tags.

The app has no database. Our server only serves the app pages and receives Shopify webhooks. It keeps no shop data, no customer data and no order data.

What the app reads at checkout

Shopify runs the app's two checkout Functions inside Shopify. For each checkout, it gives them the shipping options or payment methods on offer, the cart subtotal, item count and weight, the country and postal code of the shipping address, the chosen delivery type, whether the buyer is logged in, whether the buyer belongs to a B2B company, and whether the buyer and the cart products have the tags used in your rules. The Functions use this only to decide which methods to hide, rename or move. The Functions cannot send data anywhere, and nothing is saved.

Webhooks and logs

Our server receives the Shopify webhooks for app uninstall, plan changes and privacy requests. It checks the Shopify signature, writes the topic name and the shop domain to a short-lived log at our host (Cloudflare), and keeps no request body.

Privacy requests

Because the app keeps no customer data, there is nothing to send or delete for a customer data request or a customer redact request. When you uninstall the app, Shopify removes the app's customizations and their metafields from your store. A shop redact request needs no further action.

Payments

Shopify bills the Pro plan. We never see card or bank details.

Contact

Write to the support email on the app's Shopify App Store listing.
