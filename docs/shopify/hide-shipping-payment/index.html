Zovo Hide Shipping & Payment

Hide, rename and sort shipping options and payment methods at checkout, by cart value, address, tags or B2B.

Not on the Shopify App Store yet. This page holds the support details and the privacy policy for the listing.

What it does

Set simple rules for checkout. Hide Cash on Delivery above a cart value or for store pickup. Hide Express shipping for islands and remote postal codes. Show Bank Deposit only to B2B buyers or tagged customers. Rename a shipping option to add the delivery time, or move free shipping to the top. Rules use cart value, weight, item count, country, postal code, customer and product tags. A cart tester shows the result first. Built on Shopify Functions, so it works on every plan.

  • Hide, rename or sort any shipping option or payment method
  • Conditions on cart value, weight, country, postal code, tags and B2B
  • Cart tester shows what a buyer sees before a rule goes live
  • Never hides every option, so buyers can always check out
  • Free for 3 rules, Pro for unlimited rules at $4.99 a month
