Privacy policy for Zovo Order Limits & Min Max

Last updated 9 November 2026.

Zovo Order Limits & Min Max ("the app") is made by Zovo (theluckystrike on GitHub). This page says what data the app handles and why.

What the app stores

The app stores your limit rules in one metafield on the checkout rule it creates in your store. That metafield lives in your Shopify store, not on our servers. It holds rule settings such as quantities, order values, product and collection IDs, product tags, customer tags and postal code prefixes that you type in.

The app has no database. Our server only serves the app pages and receives Shopify webhooks. It keeps no shop data, no customer data and no order data.

What the app reads at checkout

Shopify runs the app's checkout Function inside Shopify. For each cart, it gives the Function the cart lines, the cart subtotal, whether the buyer has the customer tags used in your rules, whether the buyer belongs to a B2B company, and the shipping address lines, postal code and country. The Function uses them only to decide if a limit is hit. The Function cannot send data anywhere, and nothing is saved.

Webhooks and logs

Our server receives the Shopify webhooks for app uninstall, plan changes and privacy requests. It checks the Shopify signature, writes the topic name and the shop domain to a short-lived log at our host (Cloudflare), and keeps no request body.

Privacy requests

Because the app keeps no customer data, there is nothing to send or delete for a customer data request or a customer redact request. When you uninstall the app, Shopify removes the app's metafields from your store. A shop redact request needs no further action.

Payments

Shopify bills the Pro plan. We never see card or bank details.

Contact

Write to the support email on the app's Shopify App Store listing.
