Zovo Order Limits & Min Max

Minimum and maximum quantities, case packs, order value limits and address checks that Shopify checkout enforces.

Not on the Shopify App Store yet. This page holds the support details and the privacy policy for the listing.

What it does

Stop orders you cannot fill. Set a minimum or maximum per product or variant, sell in case packs of 6 or 12, and set a minimum order value. Limit tagged products, picked products or collections, with mix and match inside a collection. Rules run in Shopify checkout, so Shop Pay and express buttons cannot skip them. Pro adds address checks (PO boxes, house numbers, postal codes, countries) and rules for wholesale customers, B2B buyers or guests. A cart tester shows the messages first.

  • Min, max and multiples per product, per variant or across a collection
  • Minimum and maximum order value, item count and number of products
  • Runs in Shopify checkout, including Shop Pay and express buttons
  • Block PO boxes and missing house numbers, limit postal codes (Pro)
  • Free for 2 rules, Pro for unlimited rules at $5.99 a month
