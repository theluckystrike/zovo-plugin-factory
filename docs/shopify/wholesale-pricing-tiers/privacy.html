Privacy policy for Zovo Wholesale Pricing Tiers

Last updated 9 November 2026.

Zovo Wholesale Pricing Tiers ("the app") is made by Zovo (theluckystrike on GitHub). This page says what data the app handles and why.

What the app stores

The app stores each offer (tiers, product and collection IDs, product tags and the customer tags you type in) in a metafield on the discount it creates in your store. It also stores a short list of active offers in one app metafield, so the Price tiers block on your product page can show them. Both live in your Shopify store, not on our servers.

The app has no database. Our server only serves the app pages and receives Shopify webhooks. It keeps no shop data, no customer data and no order data.

What the app reads at checkout

Shopify runs the app's discount Function inside Shopify. For each cart, it gives the Function the cart lines and their prices, whether the buyer has the customer tags used in your offers, and whether the buyer belongs to a B2B company. The Function uses them only to work out the discount. It cannot send data anywhere, and nothing is saved.

The Price tiers block runs in your theme. It reads the logged-in customer's tags in the page itself to pick the right table. Nothing is sent to us.

Webhooks and logs

Our server receives the Shopify webhooks for app uninstall, plan changes and privacy requests. It checks the Shopify signature, writes the topic name and the shop domain to a short-lived log at our host (Cloudflare), and keeps no request body.

Privacy requests

Because the app keeps no customer data, there is nothing to send or delete for a customer data request or a customer redact request. When you uninstall the app, Shopify removes the app's metafields from your store. A shop redact request needs no further action.

Payments

Shopify bills the Pro plan. We never see card or bank details.

Contact

Write to the support email on the app's Shopify App Store listing.
