Zovo Wholesale Pricing Tiers

Wholesale prices by customer tag and volume discount tiers, applied in Shopify checkout.

Not on the Shopify App Store yet. This page holds the support details and the privacy policy for the listing.

What it does

Set wholesale prices for tagged customers or B2B buyers, as a percent off, an amount off or a fixed price per unit. Add quantity tiers such as buy 3, save 10% for every product, a collection, tagged products or picked products, with mix and match. Spend tiers take a percent off the order. Each offer is a Shopify automatic discount run by Shopify Functions, so it works at checkout and with Shop Pay and never edits theme code. A Price tiers block shows each buyer the right table.

  • Wholesale prices for customer tags and B2B buyers, without theme code edits
  • Quantity tiers with percent off, amount off or a fixed price per unit
  • Spend tiers on the order total, and mix and match across a collection
  • Price tiers block shows each buyer their table on the product page
  • Free offer for everyone. Pro at $9.99 a month, no revenue caps
