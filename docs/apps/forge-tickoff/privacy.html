Privacy

Tickoff Checklists for Jira runs entirely on Atlassian Forge. It has no remote back end and makes no calls outside your Jira site.

What it reads and writes

The app calls one Jira endpoint, as the signed-in user. It reads and writes the issue property tickoff-checklist of a work item (GET and PUT /rest/api/3/issue/{id}/properties/tickoff-checklist). For the paid copy feature, it reads the same property of the other work item, by its key. Because every call runs as you, you can only read checklists on work items you can see, and change them on work items you can edit.

The app stores no account IDs, names, email addresses or avatars. Item text is what your users type. Do not put secrets or personal data in checklist items.

What it doesn't do

It doesn't send data to the developer or to any third party. It uses no analytics and no tracking. It has no background jobs.

Removal

Uninstalling the app removes its Forge storage, so the templates are deleted. Checklists stay on the work items as issue properties, because they are part of your Jira data. A Jira admin can delete them with the Jira REST API (DELETE /rest/api/3/issue/{id}/properties/tickoff-checklist).
