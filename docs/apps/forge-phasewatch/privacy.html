Privacy

Phasewatch - Time in Status for Jira runs on Atlassian Forge. It has no remote back end and makes no calls outside your Jira site.

What it reads

The app calls these Jira endpoints as the signed-in user. Every call runs as you, so the app shows times only for work items you can already see.

  • GET /rest/api/3/issue/{id} reads the created date, the status and the resolution date of the open work item.
  • GET /rest/api/3/issue/{id}/changelog reads the history of the open work item. The app uses only the status changes.
  • GET /rest/api/3/status reads the status names and their categories.
  • POST /rest/api/3/search/jql runs the JQL of the report gadget.
  • POST /rest/api/3/changelog/bulkfetch reads the status changes of the work items in the report.

What it stores

Data stays in Forge app storage on Atlassian's platform.

RecordContentsKept for
Result cacheFinished times per status, status names, and for the gadget the work item keys, summaries (cut to 120 characters) and current statuses60 seconds
Status list cacheStatus ids, names and categories1 hour
Working-hours settingsCalendar or working time, working days, start and end hour, time zone90 days after the last save or use

Each record key is a SHA-256 hash that includes your Atlassian account ID. So one person's results and settings are never shown to another person. The account ID itself is not stored as a value. The app stores no names, email addresses, avatars, comments or history authors.

What it does not do

It has no scope that writes to Jira. It does not send data to the developer or to any third party. It does not use analytics or tracking.

Removal

Uninstalling the app removes its Forge storage. Cached records also expire on their own after the times above.
