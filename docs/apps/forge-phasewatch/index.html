Phasewatch - Time in Status for Jira

Phasewatch shows how long a Jira work item stayed in each status. Open a work item and the Phasewatch panel shows the time in To Do, In Progress, In Review and every other status, how many times the item went back to each status, and how long it has been in the current status. The panel is free.

Two paid features add reports and working hours. A dashboard gadget shows time in status for a list of work items from JQL, with the average and the median per status. Working hours count only the hours your team works, for example Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 in your time zone.

What you see on a work item

Open any work item and add the Phasewatch panel from the Apps button. The app reads the status history as you, so it shows only work items you can open.

  • The current status, and how long the item has been in it ("Now in In Review for 1d 3h").
  • Lead time. For a resolved item, this is the time from created to resolved. For an open item, it is the time from created to now.
  • One row per status with the total time, the share of the whole time, and the number of visits. A status that the item entered twice shows 2 visits, and the time of both visits is added up.
  • The time per status category (To do, In progress, Done).

Calendar time counts every hour of every day. A day is 24 hours, so "2d 4h" is 52 hours.

How the times are counted

Phasewatch reads the status changes in the work item history.

  • The time before the first status change belongs to the status that the item had when it was created.
  • A work item with no status change was in its current status the whole time.
  • The current status counts until now. A resolved item that is still in Done keeps adding time to Done.
  • If a history entry has a date before the created date (this can happen after an import), the app counts it from the created date and tells you.
  • If the history is longer than the app reads (5,000 changes), or if Jira is slow, the panel says that the times are partial.

Working hours (paid)

Turn on Working hours in the panel to count only working time. Click Working hours settings to set your working days, the start and end hour, and your time zone (for example Europe/Berlin). The settings are yours. Other people keep their own settings.

The app uses the time zone rules of the platform, so daylight saving time is included on the first and the last day of each period. Whole days in between count the full working window. Clocks change at night in almost every time zone, so this rule gives the same result in practice. Working time shows in hours only ("37h 30m"), because a working day has no fixed length.

Without an active subscription or trial, the panel shows "Working hours is a paid feature of Phasewatch. A Jira admin can start a free trial or subscribe in Manage apps." Switch Working hours off to see calendar time again.

The report gadget (paid)

Add the Phasewatch report gadget to a Jira dashboard. Click Edit and enter a JQL query, for example project = ABC AND resolved >= -30d. Choose the number of rows (25, 50, 100 or 200) and calendar time or working hours.

  • One row per work item with its key, summary, current status, lead time and the time in each status.
  • The 8 statuses with the most time get their own column. The other statuses are added up in an Other column.
  • An Average row and a Median row at the top. They count only the work items that spent time in that status, so an item that never entered In Review does not pull the In Review average down.

In working hours mode, the gadget uses the working-hours settings of the person who looks at the dashboard.

Speed and limits

Results are cached for 60 seconds per person. Refresh reads Jira again when the cached result is at least 10 seconds old. Each load stops reading from Jira after 18 seconds. The panel reads up to 5,000 history entries per work item. The gadget reads up to 200 work items.

Permissions and data

The app runs on Atlassian Forge. It has no outside servers and makes no calls to other sites. It asks for two scopes.

ScopeWhy
read:jira-workRead the created date, status, resolution date and status history of work items, the status list, and run the gadget JQL, as the signed-in user
storage:appKeep the 60-second result cache, a 1-hour status list cache and your working-hours settings

The app cannot change anything in Jira. See the privacy page for the details.
