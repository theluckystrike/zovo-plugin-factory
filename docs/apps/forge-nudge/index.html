Nudge Reminders for Jira

Nudge puts a Remind me action on every Jira work item. Pick a time, add a note if you like, and Jira emails you when it's due. You can include the assignee or the watchers too. All your reminders sit on one page where you can snooze, repeat or cancel them.

It's a Forge app built with UI Kit. It has no remote backend and no egress, and it only talks to Jira on your own site.

Set a reminder

Open a work item and choose Remind me from its more actions menu (the ••• button). You can also add the Reminders panel to the work item and use Add a reminder there.

Then choose when.

  • In 3 hours
  • Tomorrow at 09:00
  • Next Monday at 09:00 (a week away if today is Monday)
  • In one week at 09:00
  • Any date and time in the next 12 months

Times use your Jira time zone, and the form shows which one. So 09:00 means 09:00 where you are, and a repeating reminder stays at 09:00 when the clocks change.

You can also set these options.

  • A note of up to 500 characters. It goes into the email.
  • Who gets the email. That's you, the assignee, the watchers, or any mix. Assignee and watchers are read when the email goes out, not when you set the reminder.
  • Repeat every day, every weekday (Monday to Friday) or every week. If a weekday reminder's time falls on a Saturday or Sunday, it moves to Monday at the same time.
  • Don't send if the work item is done by then. It's on by default. Done means the status is in Jira's Done category. For a repeating reminder, it also ends the repeat.

When the email arrives

Nudge checks for due reminders once an hour. So the email can arrive up to about an hour after the time you picked, plus whatever time Jira's mail queue takes. It isn't meant for to-the-minute alarms.

Jira sends the email through its notify API, so it comes from your site's usual Jira address. The subject reads "Reminder" followed by the work item key and title. Only people who can browse the work item get it, even if they're a watcher.

If Jira is busy or down, Nudge tries again in the next hourly run. After 3 runs in a row without success, a one-time reminder is marked Failed and the list shows why. A repeating reminder skips that one email instead, shows why, and carries on at its next time. If lots of reminders fall due at the same moment and one run can't get through them all, the rest go out in the next run.

My reminders

Open Apps, then My reminders, to see everything you've set on any work item. Scheduled reminders come first, soonest at the top. Then come the ones that were sent, skipped or failed, newest first, with the reason when something went wrong.

From there you can do these things.

  • Snooze a scheduled reminder or change its time. On a repeating reminder, a snooze moves only the next email and the repeat keeps its usual time. Tick Also move the repeat to this time to change the usual time too.
  • Remind me again on one that was already sent.
  • Cancel a reminder, or delete an old one.

Each person sees only their own reminders, in the panel and on the page. Sent, skipped and failed reminders are deleted 30 days after their time.

Permissions

ScopeWhy
read:jira-workCheck that you can see the work item, show live titles and statuses in your list, and read the status before sending.
write:jira-workSend the reminder email with Jira's notify endpoint (POST /rest/api/3/issue/{id}/notify).
read:jira-userPut your display name in emails that go to the assignee or watchers.
storage:appKeep reminders in Forge storage.
report:personal-dataRun Atlassian's weekly personal data report, so reminders of closed accounts get deleted.

When you use the panel, the action or the page, Nudge calls Jira as you, so it can't show or remind you about anything you can't see. The hourly sender has no signed-in user, so it runs as the app. It reads the work item's key, title and status. When the assignee or watchers get the email, it also reads your display name. Then it sends the email.

Privacy

Nudge stores each reminder in Forge storage inside Atlassian. That's your account ID, the work item ID and key, your note, the time and time zone, who should get the email, and how sending went. It doesn't store work item titles. It reads them live from Jira when you open your list. Every week the app runs Atlassian's personal data report and deletes all reminders of closed accounts. See PRIVACY.md for the full list.

Troubleshooting

  • No email? Look at the reminder on My reminders. A Failed or Not sent label comes with the reason.
  • Wrong time? Check the time zone shown under the time picker. It comes from your Atlassian profile, or from your browser if Jira doesn't give one.
  • A reminder says the work item was deleted or can't be seen. The work item was deleted, or the app lost access to its project. Delete the reminder, or set a new one on another work item.
