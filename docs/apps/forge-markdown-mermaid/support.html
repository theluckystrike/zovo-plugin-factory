Support

Open an issue on GitHub to report a problem or ask a question about Markdown + Mermaid. We answer within 2 business days.

Include your Atlassian product (Jira or Confluence), what you did, and what you saw. Do not paste secrets, tokens or personal data in an issue, because issues are public.

For a security problem, open an issue with the title "Security" and no details. We will then contact you.
