Conversion

How rival extensions turn free users into buyers, why it works, and what Zovo can do. Every number has a mark: F fetched (URL and time), E estimate (method shown). Unverified third-party numbers are removed.

Updated 2026-11-17 20:45 (UTC+7).

Biggest insights

  1. Zovo has a reach problem first and a checkout problem second. 12 of 4,733 installs reached a buy step. 7 opened a checkout. 0 paid.
  2. At rival rates, 0 sales from 4,733 installs is still normal (0.03 to 3.5 sales expected). 0 of 7 paid checkouts is a warning: 8.4 % chance at the 29.8 % benchmark.
  3. Rivals ask at the moment of use inside the editor, sell to companies, and give full Pro for a fixed time. Hard walls sell but wreck ratings. Users punish a loss, not a price.
  4. The best 5 changes inside the sprint rules add about USD 11 to 40 a month each (mid case). Together they reach about USD 100 a month, not more. The checkout pool caps all checkout fixes at USD 164 a month.
  5. The bigger levers need the owner: a merchant of record (mid USD 83), JetBrains paid plugins (mid USD 66 from month 6) and a 7-day reverse trial (mid USD 61). A sale at USD 19 loses money.
