Hermes agents

Sprints v8 and v9, 19 to 21 Nov 2026. Hermes runs on a fast, low-cost model through the model lock. The build seat has no tokens and no access to the shell profile.

Short answer

Hermes is a good verifier and a good research lane. As a builder it works only on simple, proven jobs. Every build still needs one PASS from a verifier that is not Hermes, and a Hermes verifier never checks a Hermes build.

The BLOCKs found real faults: an old rating module, more than 1 free try per Pro tool, the trial not first in the Hex Pro panel, and wrong tool ids in Mermaid. When the codex Plus seat ran out of usage on 20 Nov, Hermes did most static checks and kept the publisher moving.

Why builds fail: each shell command was cut off after 120 s (fixed on the hermes-build2 seat with a 1,500 s limit), runs end early with a plan instead of the work, and tasks with variation (older module layouts, several fixes in one lane) confuse the model.

Research works

HRES4 to HRES9 and APIFY3 delivered complete reports with sources on time. The CONV7 funnel insight and the v9 focus on niche engineering and database tools came from this research.

Cost

About USD 11 in total for v8 and v9, USD 6.10 of it since 20 Nov 20:30 (6,911 calls). About USD 14 of the USD 25 balance is left.

Plan

  • Keep Hermes on static and real-app verification and on research.
  • Give Hermes a build only when it is one listing with a step-by-step recipe that already passed.
  • Keep new Pro tools, fixes and listings with older layouts on the main account.
  • A top-up pays off for the verifiers, especially while codex Plus is out (until 24 Nov), not for more Hermes builds.
