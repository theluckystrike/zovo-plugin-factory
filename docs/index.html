Owner steps that switch the money on checked on 7 November at 19:20, in this order

We have built 68 products: 29 VS Code, 19 Atlassian, 11 Apify, 4 JetBrains, 2 Modrinth, 1 Obsidian, 1 Shopify and 1 Roblox. Live today: 22 VS Code extensions (57 installs) and 9 public Apify Actors. The rest wait for a store review or for one of the steps below.

  1. Atlassian, about 10 minutes. 15 apps are submitted (10 paid, 5 free), all are Waiting for Atlassian and none is rejected. Agents saved 16 of 32 answers on the Partner tab and 6 of 14 on Partner - Extended. Next: answer the rows that only you can answer (MFA, alerts, key rotation, incident plan, backups, AI use), change the Security contact on each ticket to the address that has the Atlassian developer account, then click Submit for Review once. If Atlassian sends a Stripe Identity link, do it yourself. Before 12 November, make a new Atlassian API token for the Forge CLI, or agents cannot deploy fixes.
  2. Apify identity check, 5 minutes. The company certificate is approved. Only your own ID check is left: start it at verify-company.apify.com. The 9 public Actors now have names that start with the search phrase, but they stay out of Apify Store search, and payouts cannot start, until the check passes.
  3. PayPal, 2 minutes. Give a PayPal.me link. One command then adds it to every extension, Actor and site page, next to GitHub Sponsors.
  4. Obsidian, 10 minutes. Office Viewer (DOCX, PPTX and XLSX in Obsidian) passed a test in the real app, and release 0.1.1 is public. Obsidian takes no pull requests now. Sign in on community.obsidian.md, link the GitHub account theluckystrike, choose Add a plugin or theme, and enter theluckystrike/obsidian-office-viewer.
  5. Modrinth, 3 minutes. Make a new API token with 8 scopes (list in the wave10 B-MOD1 report). The old token cannot read the user or the moderator threads.
  6. Shopify, 15 minutes. Create the Partner account at partners.shopify.com and one development store. The app Zovo Hide Shipping & Payment is built and tested.
  7. Roblox, 35 minutes. Do the ID check on create.roblox.com, then publish the Backdoor & Virus Scanner plugin once from Studio.
  8. Rating notice, a decision. Every extension asks once for a rating after 5 good uses, at most 3 times. Keep it, or ship patches that switch it off by default. All 22 live listings have 0 ratings.
  9. JetBrains, nothing to do. 4 plugins are in review for up to 2 business days: Context Drift, AI Usage Meter, DOCX & PPTX Viewer and NetCDF, HDF5 & NumPy Viewer. Watch your email for the results. Agents then publish the fixed 0.1.1 builds.
  10. Open VSX, nothing to do. 17 extensions are uploaded and the namespace claim is issue 15379, open since 7 November 10:47 with no reply yet. If Eclipse staff ask a question there, answer it. After approval the 17 extensions show for Cursor and Windsurf users.
