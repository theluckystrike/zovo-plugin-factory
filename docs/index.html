Why installs do not turn into money from the Conversion page, 2026-11-17 20:45 (UTC+7)

  1. 12 of 4,733 installs reached a buy step (0.25 %). 7 people opened a checkout. 0 paid. Reach is the first problem. (F, CI7, CIS)
  2. 0 of 7 real checkouts paid. At the 29.8 % benchmark this has an 8.4 % chance (E). The checkout is the second problem. (F, CIS, Baymard)
  3. 12 of 12 people at a buy step came from a click in the editor. Web links sent 0 confirmed people. 5 of 5 rivals ask inside the working view. (F, CI7, CI1)
  4. 1.16 against 1.78 stars Users punish a loss, not a price: 290 paywall reviews against 120 price-only reviews. (F, CI8)
  5. 19 % Zovo Pro at USD 19 is 19 % of the rival median one-time price (USD 99). (F, CI3)
  6. 6 of 8 rivals show a PO, invoice or quote path. Companies pay about 2x the personal price per seat (E). (F, CI9)
  7. 3 to 4 people came from the Hex short Pro panel. The same panel on 11 other listings sent 0 (F). Hex is the only listing where the panel works. (E, MEAS2)

Price insights from the Price insights page, 20 Nov

  1. Price is not what stops people. Revenue barely changes between USD 19 and USD 59 in the model, and 0 of 11 "Not now" clicks said "Too expensive".
  2. Only the 7-day trial dialog sells. 2 of the 4 people who came from it paid; 0 of 8 from other places did. Put it on the top 30 listings.
  3. The real median one-time price for VS Code extensions is USD 31.49, not USD 99. USD 19 is underpriced, but not by 5x.
  4. Trust is weaker than value. The Pro offer scores 56 of 100 (content 78, trust 29): 4 ratings across 136 listings and 4 seller names.
  5. 84 % of the top-20 installs face a strong free alternative. The money is in database tools and engineering niches like SQLite, ELF/PE and CAN DBC.
