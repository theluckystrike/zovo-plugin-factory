Owner steps that switch the money on checked in the store dashboards on 7 November at 10:30, in this order

64 products built: 29 VS Code, 19 Atlassian, 11 Apify, 2 Modrinth, 1 Shopify, 1 JetBrains and 1 Roblox. Live today: 22 VS Code extensions (39 installs) and 9 public Apify Actors. The rest wait for a store review or for one of the steps below.

  1. Open VSX, nothing to do now. Done on 7 November: the Eclipse account is linked to GitHub, the Publisher Agreement is signed, 17 extensions are uploaded to the namespace zovo, and the namespace claim was filed as issue 15379. Eclipse staff answer in 6 hours to 3 days. If they ask a question on the issue, answer it there. After approval the 17 extensions show for Cursor and Windsurf users.
  2. Apify identity check, 5 minutes. Open verify-company.apify.com and click Start verification under your own name. The company certificate was sent for review on 6 November. Until both pass, the 9 public Actors stay out of Apify Store search and payouts cannot start.
  3. Atlassian GDPR answer, 5 minutes. 19 apps are deployed and none is public yet. On the Gists app, open the Privacy and Security tab and say which GDPR transfer mechanism covers data sent to api.github.com. Review takes about 1 to 3 weeks. If Atlassian sends a Partner Verification link (Stripe Identity), do it on your phone.
  4. JetBrains, 2 minutes. An agent fills the vendor form and uploads Context Drift. Sign in at plugins.jetbrains.com, accept the developer agreement and upload Context Drift (38 of 38 tests pass, verifier Compatible) the first time in the browser. Agents can ship the updates after that.
  5. Shopify, 15 minutes. Create the Partner account at partners.shopify.com and one development store. The app Zovo Hide Shipping & Payment is built and tested.
  6. Roblox, 35 minutes. Do the ID check on create.roblox.com, then publish the Backdoor & Virus Scanner plugin once from Studio.
  7. Rating notice, a decision. Every extension asks once for a rating after 5 good uses, at most 3 times. Keep it, or ship patches that switch it off by default. All 15 live listings have 0 ratings.
