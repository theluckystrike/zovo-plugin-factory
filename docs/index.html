Owner steps that switch the money on about 10 minutes left, in this order

  1. Open VSX, 30 seconds. In the agent browser on port 9277, open the tab "Confirm Your Email Address", tick "I am human" and click Submit. A script is waiting. It then signs the Open VSX agreement, makes the token and publishes all 19 extensions for Cursor and Windsurf users.
  2. Apify identity check, 5 minutes. Open verify-company.apify.com and click Start verification under your own name. The company certificate was sent for review on 6 November. After both pass, Actors show in Apify Store search and payouts start.
  3. Atlassian Partner Agreement, done 6 November. Vendor Zovo (283744759) is public. 15 apps were submitted for approval, 5 free and 10 paid (AskSpace, Bulk Page Operations, Epic Totals, Issue Dates Calendar, Markdown + Mermaid, Nudge, SignOff, Secret Scanner, SpaceCal, Watching Now). Review takes about 1 to 3 weeks. One answer is yours. On the Gists app, open the Privacy and Security tab and say which GDPR transfer mechanism covers data sent to api.github.com. If Atlassian sends a Partner Verification link (Stripe Identity), do it on your phone.
  4. Atlassian payment details, done 6 November. Saved on the vendor Payment tab with the company tax ID and bank account. Check it once. Filter Activity needs licensing in its manifest before it can be listed as paid.
